A closer look at the functions.

Choose a function to see it in the gallery.

  1. 01Live rates and converterSee the live rate between two currencies and convert any amount.
  2. 02Rate storiesPlain-language notes explain how a rate has changed, such as how much more your dollar buys than a year ago.
  3. 03Charts of past ratesExplore a rate over a week, a month or a year, with its open, low, high and change.
  4. 04World of currenciesCompare around 160 currencies, search by code, currency or country, and star your favourites.
  5. 05Your moneyTrack what you hold in different currencies and see its total value in your home currency.
  6. 06Rate alertsSet alerts for a rate level, a move into its yearly high or low, or a big daily move.
  7. 07Quote checkEnter the rate a bank or exchange offers to see its markup and what it would cost you.
  8. 08RateStory PremiumRemove full-screen ads with a monthly or yearly subscription; every feature works with or without it.
