Your media. None of the tracking.

A private, offline video and music player for Android. It plays the videos and songs already on your phone, with the controls of a full-featured player and none of the tracking: no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

  • 01

    Private by design

    Works fully offline. Your media, history and settings never leave the phone.

  • 02

    A complete player

    Gestures, 0.25x to 4x speed, A-B repeat, bookmarks, chapters, sleep timer, kids lock and picture-in-picture.

  • 03

    Subtitles & big sound

    Ten subtitle formats with full SSA/ASS rendering, plus volume boost, equalizer and Bluetooth delay fixing.

A closer look at the functions.

Everything the player does, on your phone and nowhere else.

  1. 01Every video in its folderA fast library that opens instantly, with NEW and Watched marks, search, sorting and hidden folders.
  2. 02Gestures that feel naturalSwipe for brightness and volume, seek with a frame preview, double-tap to skip, pinch to zoom, hold for faster playback.
  3. 03Subtitles done properlySRT, VTT, SSA/ASS and more, with styling, sync, two subtitles at once and the original fonts and effects.
  4. 04Big soundVolume boost up to 200%, equalizer, bass boost, surround, reverb, channel modes and Bluetooth delay fixing.
  5. 05A real music player tooSongs, albums, artists and folders, lyrics, playlists, a widget and a mini player.
  6. 06Pop-up and background playPicture-in-picture, a floating window over other apps, and sound that keeps going with the screen off.
  7. 07Handy toolsPrivate folder, status saver, Recycle bin, storage browser, cleaner, and backup and restore.
  8. 08In your languageEnglish plus 20 more languages, full right-to-left layout for Arabic and Urdu, and TalkBack support.

The useful details.

Made for Android

Free, with no ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions. For Android 7.0 and newer on phones, tablets and Android TV. Find ACM Player on Google Play for current availability and device compatibility.

What it does not do

No streaming, downloads, cloud or online subtitle search. It plays what is on your phone, in the formats your phone supports.

Open source

Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer), and uses libass, FreeType, HarfBuzz and GNU FriBidi for subtitles. Every library and its licence is listed in the app under Settings › Legal › Open-source licences.
