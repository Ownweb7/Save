In short

LockClock Aurum gives you alarms, timers and a stopwatch, with optional challenges to stop an alarm.

The app is free, with an optional one-time Premium purchase through Google Play. Alarms can fail, so do not rely on the app alone when timing is critical.

2. What the app does

LockClock Aurum has alarms, several timers at once and a stopwatch. A ringing alarm can appear full screen over the lock screen, and you can choose an unlock method, such as solving a maths problem, before it stops. You can choose clock faces, colours and sounds, add your own sounds, and use a home-screen widget.

3. Alarm reliability

Alarms depend on your device: it must be switched on, with the volume, Do Not Disturb, battery-saving, notifications and exact-alarm settings allowing the alarm. Updates to Android or the maker's settings can also affect alarms. An alarm may ring late or not at all. For anything where timing is critical, such as a flight, a work shift or medication, set a second alarm on another device.

4. Unlock challenges

Unlock methods are designed to help you wake up. Choose one you can complete, including when you have just woken up. Unlock methods are not a security feature.

5. Your own sounds
