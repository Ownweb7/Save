About GlowCalc

GlowCalc – Neon Calculator is a calculator built to be quick, clear and a little bit fun: every key lights up with a neon glow in the colours you choose. Three calculators in one — basic, scientific and tax (with rates for about 100 countries, US states and Canadian provinces) — plus a two-month history you can reuse or delete. No ads, no accounts, no tracking, and no internet connection needed.
