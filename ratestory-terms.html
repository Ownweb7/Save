RateStory shows exchange rates between world currencies, a converter, charts of past rates, plain-language notes about how a rate has moved, a list of currencies you hold, and optional rate alerts.

4. Accuracy of rates

  • Rates come from third-party data providers. They are mid-market, indicative rates and may be delayed, incomplete or wrong.
  • The rate a bank, card or exchange service gives you will differ, usually because of fees and margins.
  • For some currencies the providers disagree or have gaps in their history. The app hides a figure when it detects this, but it cannot catch every error.
  • Check the rate with your bank or provider before you rely on it for a transaction.
