GlowCalc – Neon Calculator · App Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

GlowCalc – Neon Calculator for Android · Developer: ODN & Sons · Effective 30 September 2026

In short: GlowCalc does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Who we are

GlowCalc – Neon Calculator ("the app") is made by ODN & Sons. This policy explains how the app handles information. For our general developer policy covering all our apps, see the ODN & Sons Privacy Policy.

Information the app stores on your phone

To work, the app saves two things in its private storage on your device:

  • Calculation history: each calculation, its result, the calculator type (basic, scientific or tax) and the time it was made. Records older than two months are hidden, and they are deleted automatically when you open the app.
  • Settings: theme, key shape, glow colours, calculator type, tax country, region and rate, angle unit and vibration.

The app never sends this information to us or to anyone else, and Android keeps it private from other apps.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect any personal or device information. It:

  • does not request internet access, so nothing you type is sent anywhere;
  • does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or file access;
  • does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools;
  • does not use your location. At first, the tax country is taken from your phone's language and region setting. This check happens only on your phone, nothing is sent anywhere, and you can change the country at any time.

Sharing and selling

The app does not send us any data, so there is nothing for us to share, sell or transfer to third parties.

Clipboard

When you long-press the display, the result shown (or what you have typed so far) is copied to your clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. The app never reads your clipboard.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Records older than two months are deleted automatically when you open the app.
  • You can delete single records, or clear all history, on the app's History page.
  • Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, removes all of its data from your phone.

Security

Your history and settings are kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. The app does not send them anywhere, so there is no data on our servers to protect.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to answer you. We do not share or sell them, and we delete them when we no longer need them, or sooner if you ask.

Children's privacy

The app can be used by people of all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to see or delete personal data about you. The app does not send us any data about you, and everything it stores is on your own phone, where you can delete it at any time. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to show or delete your emails by writing to support@allcreatormind.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Effective" date above. You can also read this policy in the app: open Settings and tap Privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Write to support@allcreatormind.com or use our contact page.
