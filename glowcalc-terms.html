2. What the app does

GlowCalc has a basic calculator, a scientific calculator and a tax calculator that adds or removes tax using rates for about 100 countries, US states and Canadian provinces, or a rate you enter. It keeps a history of your calculations for two months, and lets you choose themes, key shapes and glow colours.

3. Price

GlowCalc is free. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.
