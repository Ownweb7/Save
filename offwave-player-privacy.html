Privacy Policy

In short: Offwave Player keeps your videos, music and activity on your phone, and we collect no personal data. The app is free with ads from Google AdMob, which may process data such as your advertising ID; Premium, bought through Google Play, removes the ads.

Offwave Player is a video and music player for Android (package com.allcreatormind.offwaveplayer) made by ODN & Sons (All Creator Mind). It plays the media stored on your device, and your media and your activity in the app stay on your device. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob; Premium, bought through Google Play, removes them. For our general developer policy covering all our apps, see the ODN & Sons Privacy Policy. For the rules that apply to using the app, see the Offwave Player Terms of Use.

At a glance

  • We (ODN & Sons) do not receive, store or sell any personal data. We have no servers of our own, no account system and no analytics.
  • The app uses the internet for three things only: showing ads (Google AdMob), asking for your consent to ads where the law requires it (Google's User Messaging Platform), and buying or checking Premium (Google Play Billing). These Google services collect some data, as described below.
  • Everything else works offline: playback, your library, history, playlists, favourites and settings never leave your device, and your videos and music are never uploaded.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Free users see a small banner at the bottom of the Videos, Music and Playlists tabs, and short full-screen ads in the video player: one when a video starts and, in videos of 5 minutes or more, one or two more at breaks announced by a 5-second countdown, at most one every 2 minutes. The video pauses for the ad and plays on afterwards. When you pause a video yourself, a small ad card can also show on the player's screen until you play again or close it. Ads are never shown in Now playing, in the pop-up player, during background playback, for Private folder videos or to Premium users.
  • The ads come from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (for users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland: Google Ireland Limited). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data from your device: the advertising ID, the IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information (such as the model, Android version, language and app version), and which ads were shown and tapped. Google uses this data under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires it, Google's consent form asks you before any ad is requested whether your data may be used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices (the row is there where the law requires it). Without consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use data such as the IP address for fraud prevention.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or ask for no personalised ads, in your device's settings (Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads, depending on your Android version).
  • We see only AdMob's aggregated reports (such as how many ads were shown in each country), never data about you.
  • For Premium users the ads service is not started, no ad is requested and no ad consent is asked.

Premium (Google Play)

  • Premium removes the ads and adds features of its own. It is sold by Google Play as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, under Google Play's terms and privacy policy. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your payment details.
  • To know whether you have Premium, the app asks Google Play on your device which Premium purchases your Google account owns, and acknowledges new purchases as Google Play requires. It remembers the answer on your device, so Premium also works offline; this record is not part of Android's cloud backup or device transfer. As the seller, we see in Google Play Console the order details Google Play gives every seller (such as the order number, the product, the price and the country), not your name or payment details.
  • You manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play (Play Store › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions), or from Manage subscription on the app's Premium page.

What the app accesses on your device, and why

  • Videos and music on your device: to list and play them (stays on your device).
  • Folders you choose (Status saver, SD card or USB storage, extra scan folders): to browse, play and save media from the folders you select with the system picker (stays on your device).
  • Files you choose (a video, a subtitle or audio file, a backup file): to open the file you selected with the system picker (stays on your device).
  • Notifications: playback controls and the media notification (stays on your device).
  • Playing in the background and keeping the device awake: to keep playing while the screen is off or you use another app (stays on your device).
  • Display over other apps (only if you turn on the floating window): to show the floating video window over other apps (stays on your device).
  • Biometrics or screen lock (Private folder): to unlock the Private folder; the check is done by Android and the app never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN (stays on your device).
  • Internet and network state: for ads, the ad consent form and Google Play purchases; the network state tells the Premium check whether the phone is online (ad and purchase data goes to Google, as described above).
  • Advertising ID (free users): used by Google AdMob for ads, as described above (processed by Google).

What the app stores, only on your device

  • Playback history and resume positions, for videos and music.
  • Playlists, favourites and bookmarks.
  • Hidden lists: the folders and videos you chose to hide from the library.
  • The audio and subtitle choices you made for each video.
  • Settings, including theme, pop-up, audio, playback and library preferences, and equalizer settings and presets you save.
  • A cache of your library (names, sizes, lengths and folders) and of video thumbnails, so the app opens quickly.
  • The Private folder and its index.
  • Whether you have Premium, as Google Play last confirmed it, and which Premium features you have already tried once.
  • Your ad consent choice, kept by Google's consent form.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage. The Private folder, its index, the app database (history, playlists, favourites, hidden lists and the library cache) and the Premium record are excluded from Android's cloud backup and device transfer. Your settings may be included in your device's own Android backup if you have it turned on; that backup is handled by Android under your account, not by us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything it stored. Videos you hide stay on your device; hiding only removes them from the app's lists.

The app writes files outside its own storage only when you ask it to: screenshots (in Pictures), statuses you save, a copy of a song you set as your ringtone, notification or alarm sound (in the matching system sounds folder), and backup files (where you choose).

What is playing shows in the media notification, on the lock screen and in the music widget if you add it. Videos from the Private folder show there only as "Private video".

Links and voice search

  • Links in the app (this policy, the terms, the website, Contact us, Rate the app, Share the app, Manage subscription) open in another app you choose, such as your browser, email app or Google Play. Tapping an ad opens the advertiser's page in your browser or another app.
  • Voice search opens your phone's own speech recognition. That service turns your speech into text under its own privacy policy; Offwave Player receives only the text, and only when you use voice search.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and its ad requests are not marked as directed at children. We collect no data from anyone; Google's processing for ads is described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and in the app with an updated date.
