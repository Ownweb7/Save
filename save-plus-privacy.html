Save+ — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Save+ Android app (com.shadow.save) · Last updated: 20 September 2026

In short: Save+ has no servers of its own, no account, and no analytics, advertising or tracking. Your savings data stays on your device. We do not collect it, and we cannot see it.

1. Data stored on your device

Your savings goals and entries, the name, email address and photo you may add on the Profile screen, your selected currency, in-app backups, copies of feedback you write, and your subscription status as last reported by Google Play are stored in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see any of it, and none of it is sent to us.

Two things can copy that data off the device, both within your own accounts and never to us:

  • If Android Backup is switched on for your Google account (it is on by default on most phones), Android copies the app's data to your own Google Drive backup and restores it when you reinstall. You can turn this off in Android Settings under Google > Backup, or delete the backup there.
  • "Back Up to Device" saves a .json file into the shared Documents/ShadowSave folder on your phone, where file managers, other apps and a connected computer can read it. It stays there after you uninstall Save+ until you delete it yourself.

Reminders are local notifications that the app schedules on your device. Your reminder settings, and the reminder messages (which can mention your goals and amounts), stay on your device and are never sent to us.

2. No data is sent to us

Save+ does not send any of your data to us or to any server we run. The only online service the app uses is Google Play, and only for subscriptions: to show plan prices, to buy or restore a subscription, and to check whether your subscription is still active. Those requests are handled by the Google Play app on your device and go to Google, not to us.

Feedback is sent by email only. The feedback form opens your own email app with your message filled in, and nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We then receive it like any other email, including your email address, and use it only to reply to you and to improve the app.

3. How data is used

On-device data is used solely to run the app — displaying goals, calculating progress, rendering charts, and showing the reminders you set. We do not collect, sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising or analytics.

4. Payments

Subscription payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your card or payment details. As the seller, we can see the order records Google Play provides to developers (such as the order number, the plan and the country of purchase), which we use only for refunds, accounting and tax. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google processes payment information.

5. Third-party services

The only third-party service Save+ uses is Google Play Billing, for subscriptions. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it has no backend servers. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles Play purchases.

6. Data deletion

You can delete any goal, together with all of its entries, at any time within the app, and undo a just-added entry for a few seconds. With Premium you can also edit or delete individual entries from a goal's History or the Activity list. You can clear the name, email address and photo on the Profile screen at any time.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes the data held inside the app. Two things outlive that and are yours to delete: backup files you saved to Documents/ShadowSave (delete them in a file manager) and, if Android Backup is on, the copy in your Google account (delete it in Android Settings under Google > Backup). Back up first if you want to keep your history.

We hold none of your app data, so there is nothing of yours on a server for us to delete. To have your feedback emails deleted, contact us (see section 8).

7. Children's privacy

Save+ is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at creatormind@gmail.com or visit allcreatormind.com.

9. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase on your behalf — the data is on your device and under your control, and the sections above explain how to remove it. If you have emailed us, you may ask us at the address above to delete that correspondence.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date, and the copy inside the app will be updated in the next release. Material changes will be described in the app's release notes.
