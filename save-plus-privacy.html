Save+ — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Save+ Android app (com.shadow.save) · Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: Save+ has no servers of its own, no account, and no analytics or tracking. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which any Premium plan removes. Your savings data stays on your device. We do not collect it, and we cannot see it.

1. Data stored on your device

Your savings goals and entries, any photo you attach to a goal, your monthly top-up plans and savings challenges, the name, email address and photo you may add on the Profile screen, your selected currency, in-app backups, copies of feedback you write, and your Premium status as last reported by Google Play are stored in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see any of it, and none of it is sent to us.

Three things can copy that data off the app's own storage, all within your own device or accounts and never to us:

  • If Android Backup is switched on for your Google account (it is on by default on most phones), Android copies the app's data to your own Google Drive backup and restores it when you reinstall. You can turn this off in Android Settings under Google > Backup, or delete the backup there.
  • "Back Up to Device" and "Export Entries (CSV)" save a file into the shared Documents/ShadowSave folder on your phone, where file managers, other apps and a connected computer can read it. It stays there after you uninstall Save+ until you delete it yourself.
  • The home-screen widget, if you add one, stores the goal name, amount and progress it displays so the launcher can draw them, and shows them on your home screen where anyone looking at your phone can see them. Remove the widget to stop this.

Reminders are local notifications that the app schedules on your device. Your reminder settings, and the reminder messages (which can mention your goals and amounts), stay on your device and are never sent to us.

If you switch on the app lock, your PIN is never stored: only a scrambled (hashed) form of it is kept on the device, which cannot be read back as your PIN. If you also switch on fingerprint or face unlock, that check is performed by Android itself — Save+ is only told whether it succeeded, and never receives your fingerprint or face data.

2. No data is sent to us

Save+ does not send any of your data to us or to any server we run. Apart from ads in the free version (see Third-Party Services), the only online service the app uses is Google Play, and only for Premium purchases and ratings: to show plan prices, to buy or restore a subscription or the one-time Lifetime purchase, to check whether your Premium is still active, and — after a goal milestone, at most once per version — to show Google's own "rate this app" sheet. Those requests are handled by the Google Play app on your device and go to Google, not to us. Any rating you leave goes to Google Play, like any other Play Store review.

Feedback is sent by email only. The feedback form opens your own email app with your message filled in, and nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We then receive it like any other email, including your email address, and use it only to reply to you and to improve the app.

Sharing your year is entirely up to you. "Share your year" draws a picture on your device showing your yearly total, the months you saved in and a few counts — never your goal names or targets — and hands it to Android's share sheet, where you choose who receives it. Nothing is shared unless you pick an app and send it, and the picture never comes to us.

3. How data is used

On-device data is used solely to run the app — displaying goals, calculating progress, rendering charts, and showing the reminders you set. We do not collect, sell or share your data, and your goals and amounts are never used for advertising or analytics.

4. Payments

Payments for subscriptions and Lifetime are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your card or payment details. As the seller, we can see the order records Google Play provides to developers (such as the order number, the plan and the country of purchase), which we use only for refunds, accounting and tax. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google processes payment information.

5. Third-party services

The third-party services Save+ uses are Google Play Billing, for subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase, Google Play's in-app review sheet, and, in the free version only, Google AdMob for ads. The app contains no analytics or tracking tools of its own, and it has no backend servers.

The free version of Save+ shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. Google's ad software on your device may collect your device's IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, how you interact with ads (such as taps) and diagnostic information, and uses them for advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change that choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Your goals, entries, amounts and profile are never given to Google AdMob or to anyone else. Any Premium plan (Weekly, Monthly, Annual or Lifetime) removes ads, and for Premium users the ad software is not started at all.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Please review Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles Play purchases and reviews.

6. Data deletion

You can delete any goal, together with all of its entries, at any time within the app, and undo a just-added entry for a few seconds. Archiving a goal keeps its history but takes it off the home screen; deleting removes it outright. With Premium you can also edit or delete individual entries from a goal's History or the Activity list. You can remove a goal's photo when editing that goal, and clear the name, email address and photo on the Profile screen at any time.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes the data held inside the app. Two things outlive that and are yours to delete: backup and CSV files you saved to Documents/ShadowSave (delete them in a file manager) and, if Android Backup is on, the copy in your Google account (delete it in Android Settings under Google > Backup). Back up first if you want to keep your history.

We hold none of your app data, so there is nothing of yours on a server for us to delete. To have your feedback emails deleted, contact us (see section 8).

7. Children's privacy

Save+ is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at creatormind@gmail.com or visit allcreatormind.com.

9. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase on your behalf — the data is on your device and under your control, and the sections above explain how to remove it. If you have emailed us, you may ask us at the address above to delete that correspondence.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date, and the copy inside the app will be updated in the next release. Material changes will be described in the app's release notes.
