In short

RateStory has no account. Your currencies, amounts, holdings and alerts stay on your device and are not sent to us.

To show rates, the app connects to exchange-rate data providers. The free app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device data such as your advertising ID to show and measure ads. Premium removes the ads and is handled by Google Play.

1. Who we are

RateStory ("the app") is made by ODN & Sons (All Creator Mind) ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. Where it differs from our general ODN & Sons Privacy Policy, this policy applies to RateStory. For the rules on using the app, see the RateStory Terms of Service.

2. Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • the currencies you choose and the amounts you enter in the converter;
  • your list of holdings and their amounts;
  • your rate alerts and settings;
  • your Premium status, as last reported by Google Play.

We cannot see this information and it is not sent to us. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

3. Exchange-rate data

To show rates and charts, the app requests exchange-rate data over the internet from third-party data providers. Like any request on the internet, this reveals your device's IP address to the provider that answers it. These requests are for rate data; you do not have an account, and the app does not ask for your name or contact details.

4. Rate alerts and notifications

If you set rate alerts, the app checks rates and shows a notification on your device. This needs the notifications permission, which you can turn off at any time in Android settings.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob and its partners. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob collects information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location worked out from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google may use this information to personalise ads, depending on your settings and on where you live.

Google explains how it uses this information in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices:

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's Android settings (Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Where the law requires consent for personalised ads, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you can make or change your choice through the app's consent options.
  • With an active Premium subscription, the app does not show ads.

6. Premium and payments

Premium is a subscription sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we never receive or store your card or payment details. The app only learns whether your subscription is active. As the seller, we can see the order records Google Play provides to developers (such as the order number, the plan and the country of purchase), which we use only for refunds, accounting and tax.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your information. We do not receive your device data ourselves; the information described above is collected by Google AdMob, Google Play and the exchange-rate data providers, under their own privacy policies.

8. Children

RateStory is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your device stays there until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Information collected by Google AdMob and Google Play is kept by Google under its own policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings. If you email us, we keep your message and email address only as long as needed to answer you, and delete them sooner if you ask.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access or delete personal data about you. We do not hold your app data on our servers; for data collected by Google, use the controls in your Google account and device. To ask about any correspondence we hold, write to support@allcreatormind.com.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to ODN & Sons at support@allcreatormind.com or use our contact page.
