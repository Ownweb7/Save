Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Applies to the ACM Player app for Android (package com.allcreatormind.player), made by ODN & Sons

In short: ACM Player is a private, offline video and music player. It does not request internet access, so it cannot send or receive anything over the network. Your media, history and settings never leave your phone.

ACM Player ("the app") is made by ODN & Sons (All Creator Mind) ("we", "us"). It plays the videos and songs already on your phone, with the controls of a full-featured player and none of the tracking. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. For our general developer policy covering all our apps, see the ODN & Sons Privacy Policy.

Information we collect

None. We don't collect, receive or store any personal or device information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking SDKs, and we have no servers that the app talks to.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Your watch and listening history, including resume positions, NEW and Watched marks, and Continue watching
  • Your playlists, favourites and bookmarks
  • Your settings, including player controls, gestures, subtitle styles, equalizer presets, themes and language
  • The library cache that lets the app open instantly and refresh in the background
  • Videos you move into the Private folder, and anything in the Recycle bin

Android keeps the app's private storage out of reach of other apps. Nothing in it is ever sent to us or to anyone else, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

What the app doesn't do

  • No internet access: the app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send or receive anything over the network.
  • No data collection: no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No streaming, downloads, cloud or online subtitle search: it plays what is on your phone.

Permissions, and why

  • Videos and music (media access): to list and play your media.
  • Notifications: playback controls and the media notification.
  • Run in the background and keep awake: to keep playing with the screen off or in other apps.
  • Display over other apps (only if you turn on the floating window): to show the floating window.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock (Private folder): to unlock the Private folder. Android does the check; the app never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.

None of these permissions gives the app access to your contacts, location, microphone or accounts, and none of them involves sending anything off your phone.

Files and folders you choose

Some features work with a folder or file you pick yourself: the status saver, SD card or USB storage, a subtitle file, an external audio file, or a backup file. These are opened through Android's own picker, only when you choose them. The app does not look at any other files. Screenshots you take are saved to Pictures/ACM Player, and backups are saved to a file you choose.

Keeping and deleting your data

Because everything stays on your device, you're in control. Clear your watch history, delete playlists, bookmarks or favourites, or move videos out of the Private folder inside the app. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, removes everything it keeps. We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. You can also read this policy in the app under Settings › About.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to ODN & Sons at support@allcreatormind.com, use Contact us in the app's Settings › About, or use our contact page.
