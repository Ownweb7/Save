Premium purchases and subscriptions

Every other feature of the app is free.
  • Without Premium these limits apply: the Private folder takes up to 10 videos, the volume boost goes up to 150%, the app uses the Amber colour theme with the Light, Dark or system theme, the pop-up player opens picture-in-picture, one subtitle shows at a time and Now playing shows the cover art.
