LockClock Aurum · App Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · Applies to the LockClock Aurum app for Android (package com.lockclock.aurum), made by ODN & Sons. Versions up to 5.4 show no ads.

In short: We don't collect your personal data: your alarms and settings stay on your phone. The app goes online only for Google Play, to buy or restore Premium, and, in the free version from 5.5, for ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some information to show those ads (see Ads in the free version).

LockClock Aurum ("LockClock", "the app") is made by ODN & Sons ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. For our general developer policy covering all our apps, see the ODN & Sons Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

None. ODN & Sons doesn't collect, receive or store any personal or device information, and we have no servers that the app talks to. The app connects only to Google services:

  • Google Play billing: for the prices and what you own, when the app opens.
  • Google AdMob: in the free version, for ads.

Google handles that information under its own privacy policy, not us.

2. What stays on your phone

  • Your alarms, timers and stopwatch laps.
  • Your settings: clock face, colours, sounds and unlock method.
  • Sounds you add yourself.
  • In the free version: how many of the month's 5 alarm rings have been used, and when the last ad was shown, so ads stay occasional.

These are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. They never leave your phone, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

3. Ads in the free version

From version 5.5, the free version of LockClock shows an occasional full-screen ad from Google AdMob. Ads appear only at a natural pause in the app. They never appear on a ringing alarm, during the unlock challenge or over the lock screen. Ads are rated for teens and up.

To show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect or receive:

  • your device's Advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which Google can estimate your approximate location;
  • information about your device and the app;
  • your interactions with ads;
  • diagnostic information.

Personalised ads:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: Google's consent message asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → About → Privacy choices for ads.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Read Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Premium removes the ads. With Lifetime or Yearly, the app never loads ads.

4. What the app doesn't do

The app's alarms, timers, stopwatch, sounds and settings never need the internet, and the app sends nothing about you to us. It has no accounts, and no analytics or tracking of its own. Nothing is sold or shared by us. Apart from the Google services described above, the app talks to no one.

5. Permissions, and why

  • Notifications: to show a ringing alarm or a finished timer and, in the free version, to say when the month's alarm rings are used up.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): to ring at the exact minute you set.
  • Full-screen alarms: to show the alarm over the lock screen.
  • Running in the foreground and keeping the phone awake: to keep playing the alarm sound until you turn the alarm off.
  • Vibration: for alarms, timers and gentle feedback when you tap.
  • Starting at boot: to set your alarms again after the phone restarts.
  • Google Play billing, and internet and network status: to show Premium's prices, check what you own, buy or restore Premium and, in the free version, load ads.
  • Advertising ID: for ads in the free version (see Ads in the free version).

None of these permissions gives the app access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera or accounts.

6. Your own sounds

When you add a sound, you choose one audio file with Android's file picker. The app copies just that file into its own storage so it can play it as an alarm, and doesn't look at any other files.

The app can also play the sounds that came with your phone. It plays them from where they are and remembers only which one you chose.

7. Purchases

Premium is either a one-time in-app purchase (Lifetime) or a yearly subscription (Yearly) through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy. A subscription renews every year until you cancel it in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions). The app never sees your payment details, only whether Premium is active and with which plan.

To show prices and check what you own, the app asks Google Play each time it opens. Google's Play Billing Library may also send Google diagnostic information about billing. The app keeps only Google Play's answer, on your phone, so Premium works offline too.

8. Backups

If Android backup is turned on, your phone may include the app's alarms and settings in its backup to your Google account. You can turn that off in your phone's settings. Whether you own Premium isn't included: on a new phone, the app asks Google Play again.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app keeps stays on your device:

  • delete alarms in the app;
  • clear the app's storage in Android settings;
  • or uninstall the app to remove everything.

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over. For information Google collects for ads or purchases, use your Advertising ID controls, your Google account settings and Google's privacy policy.

10. Children

LockClock Aurum is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children. Ads in the app are rated for teens and up.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and shown in the app, with a new effective date.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to ODN & Sons at support@allcreatormind.com.
