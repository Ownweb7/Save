In short

Bond Time helps you put your phone down by covering other apps with a full-screen block for a set time. Phone, Messages, Camera and the emergency dialer stay available, and a short wind-down always ends a session.

The app is free to use. Keys and a Bond Time Plus subscription, sold through Google Play, let you end a session instantly. It is a wellbeing tool, not a safety or parental-control system.

2. What the app does

You choose a length and start a session, or set schedules that start sessions on their own on the days you pick. While a session runs, Bond Time covers other apps with a full-screen block. Phone, Messages, Camera, the emergency dialer, Android Settings and any apps you choose stay available. Family sessions start Bond Time on every phone in the room at once. The app also shows your stats and streaks, and lets you choose themes, dial colours and timer styles.

3. Ending a session

A short wind-down always ends a session, free of charge, so you are never trapped. You can also end a session instantly by spending a key, or at any time with Bond Time Plus.

  • Unused keys stay on your device. They are never backed up, so clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them, and we cannot restore them.

6. The Accessibility service

Bond Time needs you to turn on its Accessibility service. It uses the service for one purpose: to detect which app is in the foreground so it can cover other apps while a session is active. It does not read your screen content, log your activity, or send any information anywhere. You can turn the service off at any time in your device's Accessibility settings, which stops Bond Time from covering apps. See the Bond Time Privacy Policy.

7. Emergencies and messages

Phone, Messages, the emergency dialer and Android Settings are never covered, but do not rely on Bond Time in an emergency. Notifications and messages from covered apps may be missed or delayed until the session ends, so do not start a session when you need to respond to other apps.

8. Family sessions

Only start or join a family session with people who agree to take part. Family sessions connect phones directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi and need the Nearby devices permission; they may not connect on every device. Every phone in a family session can still end its session with the wind-down, a key or Bond Time Plus.

9. Limits of blocking
