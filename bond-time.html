A closer look at the functions.

Choose a function to see it in the gallery.

  1. 01Focus sessionsChoose a length and start a session; other apps step aside until it ends.
  2. 02Essentials stay closePhone, Messages and Camera stay one tap away during every session.
  3. 03Always a way outA short wind-down ends any session, so no one is ever trapped.
  4. 04A calmer screenAfter 10 seconds the timer gives way to a wallpaper; tap to see the time left.
  5. 05SchedulesSet sessions to start on their own, such as at dinner time, on the days you pick.
  6. 06Repeating sessionsChoose a start time, a length and the days a session repeats.
  7. 07StreaksKeep a streak going for every evening the phones go down.
  8. 08StatsSee your time together, session by session and day by day.
  9. 09Family sessionsStart Bond Time on every phone in the room at once, over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, with no internet needed.
  10. 10Host or joinOne phone hosts and picks the length; everyone else taps Join to start together.
  11. 11Dial colours and timer stylesChoose from eleven dial colours and four timer styles, in light or dark.
  12. 12AtmospheresSet the scene with Forest, Galaxy, Moon & Stars or Lava & Volcano.
  13. 13Bond Time PlusWith a Plus subscription, end any session instantly; the free wind-down is always there too.
  14. 14KeysBuy keys in packs of 1, 5, 10 or 50; one key ends an active session instantly.
  15. 15Private by designNo accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your history stays on your phone.

Included and Premium

Bond Time is free to use. Keys, an in-app purchase, end a session instantly, and Bond Time Plus, a subscription, ends any session instantly without keys. The short wind-down that ends a session is always available.
