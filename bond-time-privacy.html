Privacy Policy

In short

Bond Time has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers of its own and no analytics of its own. Unless you buy Ad-free, it shows banner ads from Google AdMob on its Stats, Schedules and Settings pages. Everything else it keeps stays on your phone, and nothing the Accessibility service sees is ever collected or shared.

1. What we collect

Nothing ourselves. The app sends no data to us or to any server we run. The only data that leaves your phone goes to Google: to Google AdMob for ads (section 6) and to Google Play for purchases (section 5).

2. Stored on your device

Everything Bond Time keeps stays in its private storage on this phone: your settings, presets, schedules and wall messages; the running session and its wind-down state; your session history (start time, length and how each session ended), which powers stats and streaks; any favourite contacts you add (name and number only); a wall photo you choose (a resized copy); your key balance; whether you own Ad-free, as last confirmed by Google Play; and processed Google Play purchase tokens, so a purchase is never credited twice.

3. Accessibility service

Bond Time uses Android's Accessibility service for one purpose: to detect which app is in the foreground so it can cover other apps while a session is active. Phone, Messages, Camera, the emergency dialer, Android Settings and any apps you choose are never covered. It does not read your screen content, log your activity, or send any information anywhere, and nothing it sees is ever given to Google AdMob or anyone else. You can turn the service off at any time in your device's Accessibility settings.

4. Family sessions

Family sessions start Bond Time on every phone in the room at once. Phones connect directly to each other over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through the internet.

To find family phones, Bond Time needs the Nearby devices permission (and, on older Android versions, location). It is only used while the family session screen is open.

5. Keys and Ad-free

Keys (a consumable in-app purchase; one key ends one session instantly) and Ad-free (a one-time purchase that removes ads) are sold through Google Play. Ending a session early is always free after a short wait; keys only skip the wait.

Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we never receive or store your card or payment details. The app keeps your key balance, your Ad-free status and the purchase tokens it has processed on your phone, so a purchase is never credited twice, and it asks Google Play whether you own Ad-free, so it comes back after a reinstall on the same Google account.

As the seller, we can see the order records Google Play provides to developers (such as the order number, the product and the country of purchase), which we use only for refunds, accounting and tax.

6. Ads (Google AdMob)

Unless you buy Ad-free, Bond Time shows a banner ad from Google AdMob at the bottom of its Stats, Schedules and Settings pages. Ads are never shown on the home screen, the Keys page, the session timer or the session cover, and never while a session is running.

Google's ad software on your device may collect your device's IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, how you interact with ads (such as views and taps) and diagnostic information, and uses them for advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings > About > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Your sessions, history, contacts and photo are never given to Google AdMob or anyone else. Once you own Ad-free, Bond Time stops requesting ads.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

7. Permissions, and why

  • Accessibility service: to detect which app is in the foreground and cover other apps during a session.
  • Nearby devices (and, on older Android versions, location): to find family phones, only while you host or join a family session.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): to end sessions, and start scheduled ones, on the minute.
  • Starting at boot: to keep a running session and your schedules after a restart.
  • Notifications (if you allow them): to show the countdown and tell you when a session ends.
  • Do Not Disturb access (if you switch it on): to silence notifications during a session.
  • Phone state: so the cover's Answer button can open your call screen.
  • Internet and network state: to load ads and reach Google Play.
  • Advertising ID (added by Google's ad SDK): for ads, as described in section 6.

Every permission you are asked for, except the Accessibility service, is optional.

8. Backups

Settings, presets, schedules and history are included in Android's own backup if you use it. Keys, your Ad-free status, the running session, contacts and the wall photo are never backed up; Google Play gives Ad-free back on a new phone.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything Bond Time keeps. If you use Android's backup, a copy of your settings, presets, schedules and history may remain in your Google account backup until you remove it in Android settings. We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over.

Data collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's Privacy Policy; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

10. Children

Bond Time is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date, and the copy inside the app will be updated in the next release.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to ODN & Sons at support@allcreatormind.com or use our contact page.
