In short

Bond Time collects nothing. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics or tracking, and it never sends your data to us or to anyone else.

Everything it keeps stays on your phone. Family sessions connect phones directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, without the internet, and purchases are handled by Google Play.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Bond Time has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics or tracking, and it never sends your data to us or to anyone else.

This policy is for the Bond Time app for Android, made by ODN & Sons (All Creator Mind) ("we", "us"). Where it differs from our general ODN & Sons Privacy Policy, this policy applies to Bond Time. For the rules on using the app, see the Bond Time Terms of Service.

2. Stored on your device

Everything Bond Time keeps stays in its private storage on this phone: your settings, presets, schedules and wall messages; the running session and its wind-down state; your session history (start time, length and how each session ended), which powers stats and streaks; any favourite contacts you add (name and number only); a wall photo you choose (a resized copy); your key balance; and processed Google Play purchase tokens, so a purchase is never credited twice.

3. Accessibility service

Bond Time uses Android's Accessibility service for one purpose: to detect which app is in the foreground so it can cover other apps while a session is active. Phone, Messages, Camera, the emergency dialer, Android Settings and any apps you choose are never covered. It does not read your screen content, log your activity, or send any information anywhere. You can turn the service off at any time in your device's Accessibility settings.

4. Family sessions

Family sessions start Bond Time on every phone in the room at once. Phones connect directly to each other over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through the internet.

To find family phones, Bond Time needs the Nearby devices permission (and, on older Android versions, location). It is only used while the family session screen is open.

5. Keys and Bond Time Plus

Keys (a consumable in-app purchase) and Bond Time Plus (a subscription) are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we never receive or store your card or payment details. The app keeps your key balance and the purchase tokens it has processed on your phone, so a purchase is never credited twice, and it checks with Google Play whether your Plus subscription is active. As the seller, we can see the order records Google Play provides to developers (such as the order number, the product and the country of purchase), which we use only for refunds, accounting and tax.

6. Permissions, and why

  • Accessibility service: to detect which app is in the foreground and cover other apps during a session.
  • Nearby devices (and, on older Android versions, location): to find family phones, only while the family session screen is open.
  • Exact alarms: to end sessions, and start scheduled ones, on time.
  • Starting at boot: to restore a running session or your schedules after the phone restarts.
  • Notifications (if you allow them): to show the status of a session.

7. Backups

Settings, presets, schedules and history are included in Android's own backup if you use it. Keys, the running session, contacts and the wall photo are never backed up.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything. If you use Android's backup, a copy of your settings, presets, schedules and history may remain in your Google account backup until you remove it in Android settings. We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over.

9. Children

Bond Time has no ads and collects no data from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to ODN & Sons at support@allcreatormind.com or use our contact page.
