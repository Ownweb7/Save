In short

Will Impulse puts a lock screen in front of apps you choose, to help you pause before an impulse food order. Getting past it takes a quiz, a wait or keys.

Keys are optional in-app purchases through Google Play. Will Impulse is a self-help tool, not medical or nutritional advice.

2. What the app does

You choose apps to lock. When you open a locked app, a lock screen appears. You can unlock it for a while by answering a quiz (the difficulty you pick sets how many correct answers are needed and how long the app stays open), by waiting out a timer, or by spending keys. Removing a lock for good takes more: a longer quiz, a longer wait, or keys. The app also shows the orders you avoided, an estimate of money saved, streaks and medals, and an Emergency tab with ideas for quick home food.

3. A commitment you choose

Will Impulse is designed to be hard to get round, on purpose. While the shield is on, Android's uninstall screen is blocked for Will Impulse and for locked apps, and uninstalling Will Impulse costs keys. Locks follow an app's package name, so a locked app that is removed and reinstalled is locked again. If you turn on device admin, Android itself refuses to remove the app.

Before you lock an app, make sure you will not need it urgently. Do not lock apps you may need in an emergency or for work, health, travel or money, such as banking, maps, ride or medical apps.

  • Your key balance is stored on your device. If you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, unused keys may be lost and we may not be able to restore them.

5. Not medical or nutritional advice

Will Impulse is a self-help tool. It is not medical, nutritional or psychological advice or treatment. Food ideas in the Emergency tab are general suggestions; check them against your own allergies and dietary needs. "Money saved" figures are estimates based on the per-order amount you set. If you are worried about your eating habits, speak to a qualified professional.
