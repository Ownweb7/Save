Who we are

ODN & Sons is a boutique mobile application studio founded in 2022 and registered in Hisar, India. We are the developer of Save+, Bond Time, Will Impulse, GlowCalc and LockClock Aurum on the Google Play Store, united by a single belief: great software should feel effortless. No clutter, no confusion — only refined experiences that respect your time.

From the first sketch to the final release, every product carries the same hallmark of quality. We favour clarity over complexity and craft over noise, so that whichever ODN & Sons app you open, it feels considered, premium and genuinely useful.

Our apps

Save+ helps you set and track your saving goals — a simple, private way to watch your progress grow. Explore the app → · Privacy policy

Bond Time helps families be present together without phones stealing the moment. Set a duration and the phone is blocked for that time — only Phone, Messages and Camera stay usable, so you never miss a call or a memory. It works fully offline, with no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Explore the app → · Privacy policy

Will Impulse locks the apps that trigger your impulse food orders. Open a locked app and a lock screen stands in the way — getting past it takes a quiz, a wait, or keys, so an order only happens when you truly want it. Everything stays on your phone. Explore the app → · Privacy policy

GlowCalc – Neon Calculator is a calculator built to be quick, clear and a little bit fun: every key lights up with a neon glow in the colours you choose. Three calculators in one — basic, scientific and tax (with rates for about 100 countries, US states and Canadian provinces) — plus a two-month history you can reuse or delete. No ads, no accounts, no tracking, and no internet connection needed. Explore the app → · Privacy policy

LockClock Aurum is an alarm clock, timer and stopwatch with a lock-screen alarm and customisable clock faces, colours and sounds — including sounds you add yourself. It has no internet permission, so nothing ever leaves your phone: no accounts, no analytics, no ads. Explore the app → · Privacy policy

What we believe

We build for people, not features. That means thoughtful design, honest functionality and a deep respect for your privacy. Our apps are intentionally simple to use — powerful underneath, calm on the surface.

Our approach is simple: useful tools, care in every detail, and clear policies. Explore our approach below.
