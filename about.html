Who we are

ODN & Sons is a boutique mobile application studio founded in 2022 and registered in Hisar, India. We are the developer of Save+, Bond Time, and a growing family of apps on the Google Play Store, united by a single belief: great software should feel effortless. No clutter, no confusion — only refined experiences that respect your time.

From the first sketch to the final release, every product carries the same hallmark of quality. We favour clarity over complexity and craft over noise, so that whichever ODN & Sons app you open, it feels considered, premium and genuinely useful.

Our apps

Save+ helps you set and track your saving goals — a simple, private way to watch your progress grow. App privacy policy →

Bond Time helps families be present together without phones stealing the moment. Set a duration and the phone is blocked for that time — only Phone, Messages and Camera stay usable, so you never miss a call or a memory. It works fully offline, with no accounts, no ads and no tracking. App privacy policy →

Will Impulse locks the apps that trigger your impulse food orders. Open a locked app and a lock screen stands in the way — getting past it takes a quiz, a wait, or keys, so an order only happens when you truly want it. Everything stays on your phone. App privacy policy →

GlowCalc – Neon Calculator is a calculator built to be quick, clear and a little bit fun: every key lights up with a neon glow in the colours you choose. Three calculators in one — basic, scientific and tax (with rates for about 100 countries, US states and Canadian provinces) — plus a two-month history you can reuse or delete. No ads, no accounts, no tracking, and no internet connection needed. App privacy policy →

LockClock Aurum is an alarm clock, timer and stopwatch with a lock-screen alarm and customisable clock faces, colours and sounds — including sounds you add yourself. It has no internet permission, so nothing ever leaves your phone: no accounts, no analytics, no ads. App privacy policy →

What we believe

We build for people, not features. That means thoughtful design, honest functionality and a deep respect for your privacy. Our apps are intentionally simple to use — powerful underneath, calm on the surface.

Simplicity

Every interaction is stripped back to what truly matters, so anyone can pick up our apps and feel at home immediately.

Craft

We sweat the details — the motion, the spacing, the finish — to deliver a premium feel in everything we ship.

Trust

One transparent privacy policy and a clear set of terms apply across all our apps. What you see is what you get.

Looking ahead

ODN & Sons is built for the long term. This site is the shared home for our entire collection of applications — those available today and every one we create in the future. As the family grows, our commitment stays the same: elegant, user-friendly apps you can rely on.
