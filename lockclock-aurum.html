About LockClock Aurum

LockClock Aurum is an alarm clock, timer and stopwatch with a lock-screen alarm and customisable clock faces, colours and sounds — including sounds you add yourself. It has no internet permission, so nothing ever leaves your phone: no accounts, no analytics, no ads.
